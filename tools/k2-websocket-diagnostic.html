Creality Monitor - connection diagnostic

This page checks one printer's status WebSocket from this browser and records every status field the printer reports. It does not scan your network, open the camera, or send print, fan, light, calibration or G-code commands. Tokens, passwords, Wi-Fi names, MAC/serial numbers and addresses are masked.

Uses ws://<IP>:9999/ and sends the same 5-second heartbeat used by the Creality Monitor widgets and Stream Deck plugins. Keep this page open until the test finishes. If Chrome asks for permission to connect to devices on your local network, allow it.
